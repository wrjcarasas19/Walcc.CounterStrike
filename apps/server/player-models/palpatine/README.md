# Emperor Palpatine

"Emperor Palpatine / Darth Sidious" by Mind Mulch for The Masses
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/emperor-palpatine-darth-sidious-c00a201b49ac46cc8a95c828b76865ba),
licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Files, unchanged from the Sketchfab download (glTF):

- `Unity2Skfb.gltf`, `Unity2Skfb.bin`: the mesh and its Mixamo skeleton, from `source/Unity2Skfb.zip`
- `Darth_Sidious_D.jpeg`: the texture, from `textures/`

Changes in the image: `../gltf2mdl.py` puts the mesh on the stock Counter-Strike
SEAL Team 6 skeleton (`models/player/urban/urban.mdl`) and its animations,
binds each vertex to a single bone and reduces the texture to 512×512 pixels
and 256 colours.

Star Wars and its characters belong to Lucasfilm Ltd.
